# Billing FAQ

Invoices are generated automatically on the first day of each billing cycle and emailed to the account's billing contact. A copy is always available in Account Settings under Billing History.

Refunds for a canceled subscription are prorated to the day of cancellation and are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

To update a payment method, the customer must be the account owner or have billing permissions. Payment method changes take effect immediately and apply to the next billing cycle; they do not retroactively affect an invoice that has already been charged.

Failed payments are retried automatically three times over seven days. After the third failed attempt, the account is downgraded to the free tier and the customer is notified by email with instructions to update payment details.

Disputed charges should go through the customer's bank or card issuer only after contacting billing support first; most disputes are resolved faster through a direct refund than a chargeback.

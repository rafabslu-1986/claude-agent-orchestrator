# Product Plans

The Starter plan is billed monthly and includes core features with a fixed monthly usage cap. It is intended for individuals and small teams evaluating the product.

The Growth plan is billed monthly or annually, with a 15% discount for annual billing. It raises the usage cap significantly and adds priority email support with a one-business-day response target.

The Enterprise plan is custom-priced and requires talking to sales. It includes a dedicated account manager, a custom usage cap, single sign-on (SSO), and a service-level agreement (SLA) with guaranteed uptime and response times.

Any plan can be upgraded at any time and the change takes effect immediately, with the price difference prorated for the current billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle so the customer keeps access to what they already paid for.

A 14-day free trial of the Growth plan is available to new accounts and does not require a payment method to start.

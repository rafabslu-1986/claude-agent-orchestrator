# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days within the country of origin. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and is available at checkout for an additional fee.

International orders take 10 to 21 business days depending on customs processing in the destination country. Customs duties and import taxes, if any, are the customer's responsibility and are not included in the order total.

Once an order ships, the customer receives a tracking number by email within 24 hours. Orders cannot be redirected to a different address after they have shipped; a new order must be placed instead.

Lost packages are investigated with the carrier. If a package is confirmed lost in transit, we ship a free replacement or issue a full refund, whichever the customer prefers.
